# Module 5. Characteristics of X-Rays and Particle Beams

Original items. Superficial and orthovoltage x-rays versus electrons for skin, and the opposite trends of surface dose with energy for electrons and megavoltage photons.

## Choosing a Skin Beam and Surface Dose Numbers

### Q1
Stem: A 1 cm thick skin lesion lies over the tibia. Compared with orthovoltage x-rays, treating it with a 9 MeV electron beam plus bolus mainly:
A. Raises the surface dose from about 85% to 100% without changing the bone dose
B. Lowers the dose to the underlying bone and stops the dose beyond the practical range
C. Removes the need for bolus because electrons already deposit 100% at the surface
D. Increases the dose to the bone because electrons are stopped by high-Z material
Answer: B
Objective: I.3
Why: Orthovoltage is photoelectric-dominated, so bone absorbs several times more dose per gram than soft tissue and the beam has no sharp end; electrons deposit nearly the same dose per gram in bone as in soft tissue and stop at about E/2 cm. Bolus is needed precisely because the electron surface dose at 9 MeV is only about 85%, so options A and C invert the roles. Electrons are not preferentially absorbed by bone.

### Q2
Stem: Which beam has a percent surface dose of 100% with no buildup region?
A. 250 kVp orthovoltage
B. 6 MeV electrons
C. Co-60 gamma rays
D. 6 MV photons
Answer: A
Objective: I.3
Why: At kilovoltage energies the secondary electrons have negligible range, so the dose is maximal at the surface and falls off from there; this is why superficial and orthovoltage beams are prescribed at the skin. A 6 MeV electron beam enters at about 75% and peaks a centimeter deep. Co-60 and 6 MV photons have a buildup region with dmax at 0.5 and 1.5 cm and surface doses of roughly 15 to 20% and 10 to 15%.

### Q3
Stem: The percent surface dose of a clinical electron beam increases from about 75% at 6 MeV to about 95% at 18 MeV because:
A. Higher-energy electrons produce more bremsstrahlung at the surface
B. The practical range becomes shorter at higher energy
C. Lower-energy electrons scatter through larger angles, so fluence builds up more before dmax
D. The flattening filter hardens the higher-energy beam
Answer: C
Objective: I.3
Why: Low-energy electrons are deflected through large angles in the first millimeters of tissue, so the planar fluence and therefore the dose increase from the surface to dmax; high-energy electrons travel almost straight and the surface dose is already near the maximum. Bremsstrahlung is a small tail beyond the range, not a surface effect. The practical range lengthens with energy. Electron beams use scattering foils, not flattening filters.

### Q4
Stem: For a 10 × 10 cm open field, which ordering of percent surface dose from highest to lowest is correct?
A. 18 MV photons, 6 MV photons, Co-60, 18 MeV electrons
B. 18 MeV electrons, 6 MeV electrons, 6 MV photons, 18 MV photons
C. 6 MeV electrons, 18 MeV electrons, 18 MV photons, 6 MV photons
D. 6 MV photons, 18 MV photons, 6 MeV electrons, 18 MeV electrons
Answer: B
Objective: I.3
Why: Electron surface dose rises with energy (about 95% at 18 MeV, about 75% at 6 MeV) and megavoltage photon surface dose falls with energy (roughly 10 to 15% at 6 MV, under 10% at 18 MV) because the secondary electrons become more forward-directed and the buildup region lengthens. Option A reverses the photon trend and puts photons above electrons. Option C reverses both trends within each pair. Option D puts photons above electrons.

### Q5
Stem: Which change lowers the surface dose of a megavoltage photon field?
A. Switching from a flattened to a flattening-filter-free beam
B. Adding a blocking tray 15 cm from the skin
C. Increasing the energy from 6 MV to 15 MV for the same field size
D. Opening the field from 10 × 10 cm to 30 × 30 cm
Answer: C
Objective: I.3
Why: Raising the photon energy lengthens the buildup region and lowers the entrance dose for an open field. Removing the flattening filter softens the spectrum and lets more contaminant electrons through, which raises surface dose, as does a tray near the skin that generates electrons, and a larger field that adds scattered electrons from the collimator and air.
