# Open sample tests

The Medical Dosimetrist Certification Board publishes practice tests made of retired items. Those items are copyrighted by the MDCB. They are not copied into this repo. Stems, options, and answer keys are not reproduced here.

The practice-tests page says the items reflect the test matrix, do not cover every subcategory, may contain detail that is no longer on the exam, and do not predict a CMD result.

https://www.mdcb.org/certification-exam-information/practice-tests

The September 2026 handbook points to a nested path, `/certification-exam-information/exam-dates-and-fees/practice-tests`. The live page and the January 2027 applicant checklist use:

https://mdcb.org/certification-exam-information/practice-tests

The January 2027 handbook is the newer sitting document. The live page is the one that resolved on 2026-10-10.

## Topic index

This is a skill index for what those manuals are used to sample. It is not a transcription of items. The PDFs were not opened on this pass. The skill list matches the index already kept in `knowledge/plan/mdcb-objective-map.md`.

- Cumulative and differential DVH, and what a DVH cannot show (location, contour quality, dose-grid size)
- Applying a QUANTEC constraint to a stated DVH point
- Electron rules of thumb, Bentel and Khan, plus energy loss of about 2 MeV/cm and practical range of about E/2
- Which algorithm handles interface dose (Monte Carlo, convolution, pencil beam)
- Helical tomotherapy, step-and-shoot, and sliding window (monitor units and time)
- Thermal-neutron shielding (boronated polyethylene)
- What raises megavoltage skin dose (bolus, tray distance, obliquity, carbon-fiber couch)
- Photon-electron junction hot and cold spots
- Adjacent electron fields and surface cold spots
- Physical penumbra, 80% to 20% at dmax, and the geometric penumbra formula
- Breast-tangent divergence, collimator angle, couch kick, and half-beam block
- Mayneord F as an inverse-square correction only
- Clarkson separation of primary and scatter
- Equivalent square 2ab/(a+b), and why percent-blocked area is an incomplete scatter correction
- SSD and SAD monitor-unit geometry, with the calibration condition stated in the item
- Inverse square for dose rate and distance
- Given dose and tumor dose
- A wedge factor left out of the monitor units while the wedge remains in the beam
- Half-life from two assays
- Permanent-implant total dose = initial dose rate × 1.44 × half-life
- Magnification = source-to-film / source-to-object
- NCRP 116 occupational and public limits, pregnancy, the linear no-threshold model, and warning-sign thresholds
- TG-142 cadence (daily interlock, output tolerances) and the weekly chart check
- DICOM as the transfer standard
- TG-43 quantities and point A
- Exposure rate, X = Γ A / d²
- Autoradiography of a source
- Mechanical isocenter (gantry rotation about an independent pointer)
- Field-in-field for hot spots
- SBRT margin and steep falloff
- Lead thickness for electrons, about E/2 mm
- Internal eye shields limited to low electron energies
- Proton treatment energies about 150–250 MeV, and RBE 1.1
- BED and EQD2
- Pacemakers and high-energy neutrons (TG-203)
- Density override for contrast and metal
- Dose-grid limits and deformable-registration dose accumulation
- AAMD scope of practice and RO-ILS
- Clinical-trial phases and binary-to-decimal conversion (bonus study only; not a 2023 objective domain)
