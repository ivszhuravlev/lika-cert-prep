## What the items test

A small number of performance-based items are on the CMD exam. They include multiple-choice items and contouring items. They test plan evaluation, contouring, isodose distributions, dose to critical structures, DVHs, anatomical structures for site-specific treatments, and beam arrangements.

The candidate uses the email address on the MDCB application. At mdcb.proknow.com, enter that email, leave the password blank, and use “click here to set or reset your password.” The account is separate from any other ProKnow site. Set it up after approval. Technical problems go to info@mdcb.org, with a screenshot. ProKnow staff do not manage mdcb.proknow.com. For the January 2027 exam, log in for technical help no later than January 7.

The tutorial is turned off 72 hours before the first exam day. After that, and until the appointment is over, the candidate does not log in. The site is monitored. A login in that window can invalidate the score under Ethical Standard 6. Bring the login and password to the center. See `exam-administration.md`.

## The button

On a multiple-choice performance item, a button on the left, just above options A–D, opens the patient data set in ProKnow. Maximize that window after it loads. The board says the data load in 10–15 seconds. If the CT opens black or missing, check window and level. The window can be resized to move between the data set and the question. The answer is selected in the exam, not in ProKnow.

Close open contour tabs as each item is finished so the scans are not mixed. After the ProKnow items, log out from the logo in the upper left.

File and account settings stay as they are. On a contouring item, do not delete the patient. A deleted patient is not restored during the exam.

## Multiple-choice skills checklist

The handbook says dose is displayed in absolute dose (Gy).

1. Log in to mdcb.proknow.com.
2. Turn all contours on or off, and turn individual contours on or off.
3. Turn all doses on or off, and turn individual doses on or off. Add a dose level, or change a dose level that is displayed.
4. Scroll axial, sagittal, and coronal images with the keyboard and the mouse.
5. Pan and zoom in all three planes.
6. Adjust window and level, and reset window and level.
7. Use the probe tool for distance and for dose profiles.
8. View the DVH. Turn all structures on or off, and turn individual structures on or off.
9. Hide or show DVH statistics: minimum, maximum, mean, and volume.
10. Change the DVH among cumulative dose, differential dose, relative, and absolute.
11. Leave file and account settings unchanged.

## Contouring skills checklist

Practice data set named in the handbook: `Spine_01_contouring`.

https://mdcb.proknow.com/tutorialcontouring/patients

1. Log in to mdcb.proknow.com.
2. Open `Spine_01_contouring`.
3. Open the Structures tab.
4. Choose Edit Structure Set.
5. If the item names a bounding box, find it. Contouring superior or inferior to the box is not penalized.
6. Set window and level for the contour.
7. Contour only the structure labeled “structure.” Create no new structure.
8. Draw with the draw tool, with or without smooth lines.
9. Paint with any brush diameter.
10. Erase with the draw or paint tool.
11. Hold Shift or Alt and draw or paint outside or inside a contour to add another contour on the slice or to make a hole.
12. Interpolate between slices.
13. Copy from the inferior or superior slice.
14. Clear the current slice, or clear all slices.
15. Select Commit twice to submit and leave ProKnow.

The handbook’s practice task on that data set is to contour the sternum on the structure labeled “structure,” inside the bounding box on axial slices from −10.0 mm to 40.0 mm. No DVH is provided for that practice patient. That task is a handbook exercise, not an exam item, and the exam stems are not copied here.
