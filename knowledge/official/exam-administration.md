## Exam sitting

- 155 questions. Time allowed: 3 hours 50 minutes. The arrival section also states the total test time as 230 minutes.
- English only. Written for U.S. practice.
- A small percentage of randomly embedded unscored items are mixed in. They are omitted from the score.
- A small number of items are performance-based in ProKnow. See `performance-based-testing.md`.
- Pass/fail against a cut score. A passing result is a scaled score of at least 600. A passing mark in each domain is not required.
- Vendor: Meazure Learning. Computer-based centers in the United States and Canada, plus other international sites. Scheduling support: testingsupport@meazurelearning.com and https://support.meazurelearning.com/hc/en-us
- Authorization to test arrives by email about four weeks after the status notice. The registration number is the 7-digit MDCB number.

## Fees

Dates page and the January 2027 handbook match on the three published fees.

| Fee | Amount | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exam | $375 | Every applicant and reapplicant, each attempt |
| Application | $200 | Every applicant and reapplicant |
| Late | $75 | After the early deadline, through the late deadline |

- Credit card, U.S. dollars. Debit cards are not accepted.
- Dates page: all fees are non-refundable once the application has been submitted for review.
- Handbook, same sitting: no refund for any reason once payment has been submitted, including ineligible, withdrawn, and no-show applications.
- No-show fee on the next application: $250. The handbook applies this if the candidate does not withdraw at least 72 hours before the scheduled start, or arrives 15 minutes or more after the start.
- Approved candidates who withdraw get no refund. The next application adds a $125 rescheduling fee on top of the application and exam fees.
- Withdrawal is an email to info@mdcb.org with the subject “Intent to Withdraw,” plus a cancellation with Meazure.
- Reschedule or withdraw up to one week before the first test date.
- A candidate who does not appear, or who tries to cancel less than two calendar days before the appointment, is a no-show.
- A no-show or a withdrawal is not an exam attempt.

## Eligibility routes in the January 2027 handbook

First-time applicants, Route 1:

- Graduate of a U.S. JRCERT-accredited medical dosimetry program of at least 12 months, and hold a bachelor’s degree.
- Submit an official diploma, a matching official transcript, and a certificate of completion of clinical requirements.
- A certificate program also requires the official bachelor’s diploma. A non-English bachelor’s diploma needs an English translation.
- Names on the documents match the application, or an official name-change decree is included.
- If the official diploma cannot be supplied by the deadline, a program-director letter on official letterhead can stand in for the diploma only. The graduation date on the letter matches the transcript.
- For the January 2027 exam, the clinical certificate is signed by the program director. Beginning with the September 2027 exam, that certificate must be on the MDCB template issued to program directors.
- Program list: http://www.jrcert.org/

The applicant-handbook page says clinical-training documentation, as a certificate from the dosimetry program, has been required beginning with the January 2026 exam. The January 2027 handbook keeps that requirement and adds the MDCB template starting September 2027.

The dates page describes eligibility as one route, JRCERT graduation. The January 2027 handbook is the sitting’s administration document and still prints Route 2 for one reapplicant group.

Reapplicants:

- A full new application is required if the prior application preceded the September 2011 exam.
- Route 1 reapplicants: 8 MDCB-approved CEUs, not previously submitted, completed within 12 months of the reapplication deadline.
- Route 2 is limited to second and third attempts whose original application was before 2011 and whose education credential was ARRT (or a foreign equivalent), a bachelor’s degree, or a bachelor of science. Those candidates may submit that same credential again on the Route 2 application.
- Route 2 reapplicants: 24 MDCB-approved CEUs, not previously submitted, earned during the clinical experience and within the three years before the application deadline.
- Route 2 clinical experience: at least 36 months, or 35 hours per week full-time equivalent (5,460 hours), creating clinically deliverable plans under a CMD in active status or a medical physicist. The work must fall within the six years before the application-deadline year. Two references (CMD or physicist), with work email addresses, are required for each job. The board contacts the references.
- Foreign bachelor’s credentials for that Route 2 path: evaluation from Foundation for International Services (FIS) or International Education Research Foundation (IERF) only.

Three-attempt rule, as printed for this sitting:

- Beginning with the January 2026 exam, a candidate who fails has five years to complete up to two more attempts. After the third failure, the candidate is ineligible for two calendar years.
- Candidates whose first attempt preceded January 2026 are outside that five-year restriction and must document the earlier attempts.
- After the two-year wait, the person applies as a new candidate under the eligibility rules then in effect and submits 8 MDCB-approved CE credits.
- After a failure, the candidate sits the next two successive administrations under the route already approved. If the exam is offered more than once in a calendar year, the candidate may sit each administration.

The September 2026 handbook states that residents of a country or region under a U.S. embargo are not eligible. That sentence was not re-checked in the January 2027 file.

## What to bring, and the room

Know-before-you-go list in the January 2027 handbook:

- A valid government-issued photo ID. The test-center rule adds that the ID is original, unexpired, and bears a photo and a signature, and that the name matches the application.
- The Meazure scheduling confirmation.
- The ProKnow login and password.
- A non-programmable scientific calculator.
- Noise-reduction earphones.

Arrival is 30 minutes before the start. Arrival more than 15 minutes after the start is a refusal. There is no scheduled break. Candidates may leave the room only under the center’s sign-out rules and may not leave the building. The locker is for food, drink, or medicine. Repeated or long absences are reported to the MDCB.

Calculator: silent, hand-held, solar or battery. Alpha, programmable, and paper-tape calculators are excluded. Meazure does not supply one. Borrowing is excluded. A calculator outside the rule is grounds for dismissal. Named examples: Casio fx-115ES Plus, Casio fx-991CW, Sharp EL-W516T, Sharp EL501X2, TI-30XS Multiview, TI-36X Pro. Any non-programmable, paper-tape-free calculator is allowed. Calculators are checked.

The center may issue one erasable note board. It is returned at the end or a score report is not issued. Written notes are excluded, except a note of the ProKnow login. The September 2026 handbook says the administrator will allow the candidate to record the login and password before being seated. The January 2027 extract confirms the instruction to bring the credentials. Personal ear plugs may be inspected. Centers do not all supply them.

Prohibited in the room: cameras and phones, notes and books, bags, purses, media players, tablets, headphones, pagers, computers and other devices with memory, personal pens and pencils, electronic glasses, watches and smart devices, jewelry other than a wedding or engagement ring, weapons, medicine including cough drops unless permitted in advance, food and beverages, coats, and hats or hoods unless required for religion. Sweaters and sweatshirts without pockets or hoods may be worn. Masks follow the local center rule.

## Score validity

These acts can void a score or end the sitting:

- Logging into mdcb.proknow.com after the tutorial is turned off, and before or after the exam appointment. The handbook names this as MDCB Ethical Standard 6. The tutorial is turned off 72 hours before the first exam day. The site is monitored. For this sitting, candidates are told to log in for technical help no later than January 7, and to email info@mdcb.org with a screenshot.
- Misconduct reported by the test-center administrator, including abusive conduct. The administrator may dismiss the candidate. The vendor and the MDCB may invalidate the score and may pursue civil or criminal charges.
- Disclosure or reconstruction of any exam item. The exam is proprietary. The board may cancel a score when security, statistics, or observations put the score in doubt.
- A calculator outside the published rule.
- Fraud in the application, which the handbook lists as grounds for denial or revocation of the certificate.

The wording of Ethical Standard 6 itself is on http://www.mdcb.org/about-mdcb/ethical-standards . That page was not retrieved on this pass. The score consequence above is the handbook’s statement.
