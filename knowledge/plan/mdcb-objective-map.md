# MDCB 2023 test specification, mapped onto the public plan

Source of the objectives and weights: MDCB CMD Test Specifications Matrix, 2023 Job Task Analysis, as printed in the public exam handbook.

https://mdcb.org/sites/mdcb/files/images/MDCB%20CMD%20Test%20Specifications%20Matrix_JTA%202023.pdf

https://mdcb.org/sites/mdcb/files/docs/2026/Exam%20Handbook_2026.9_final.pdf

The exam has 155 questions, 3 hours 50 minutes, English only, U.S. practice.
A few unscored pilot items are mixed in.
A small number of items are performance-based in ProKnow (plan evaluation, isodose, DVH, anatomy, beam arrangement, contouring).

## Skills the public sample-test manuals are built to sample

MDCB publishes retired items so candidates can see format and difficulty.
Those items are not stored here.
The original question files must still be able to test every skill those manuals are known to sample:

- Cumulative vs differential DVH, and what a DVH cannot prove (location, contour quality, grid size)
- Applying a QUANTEC constraint to a stated DVH point
- Electron rules of thumb, both Khan and Bentel, plus energy loss about 2 MeV/cm and practical range about E/2
- Which algorithm handles interface dose (Monte Carlo vs convolution vs pencil beam)
- Helical tomotherapy vs step-and-shoot vs sliding window (MU and time)
- Thermal neutron shielding (boronated polyethylene)
- What raises megavoltage skin dose (bolus, tray distance, obliquity, carbon fiber couch)
- Photon-electron junction hot and cold spots
- Adjacent electrons and surface cold spots
- Physical penumbra 80% to 20% at dmax; geometric penumbra formula
- Breast tangent divergence, collimator angle, couch kick, half-beam
- Mayneord F is an inverse-square correction only
- Clarkson separates primary and scatter
- Equivalent square 2ab/(a+b), and why percent-blocked area is not a full scatter correction
- SSD vs SAD monitor-unit setups, with the calibration condition stated in the stem
- Inverse square for dose rate vs distance
- Given dose vs tumor dose
- Wedge factor omitted from the MU and the wedge still in the beam
- Half-life from two assays; permanent implant total dose = initial dose rate × 1.44 × half-life
- Magnification = source-to-film / source-to-object
- NCRP 116 occupational and public limits, pregnancy, LNT, warning-sign thresholds
- TG-142 cadence (daily interlock, output tolerances) and weekly chart check
- DICOM as the transfer standard
- TG-43 quantities and point A
- Exposure rate X = Γ A / d²
- Autoradiography of a source
- Mechanical isocenter (gantry rotation about an independent pointer)
- Field-in-field for hot spots
- SBRT margin is tight because falloff is steep
- Lead thickness for electrons about E/2 mm
- Internal eye shields only for low electron energies
- Proton treatment energies about 150–250 MeV, RBE 1.1
- BED and EQD2
- Pacemaker and high-energy neutrons (TG-203)
- Density override for contrast and metal
- Dose-grid and DIR accumulation limits
- AAMD scope, RO-ILS
- Trial phases and binary conversion (bonus module only)
