Radioactivity

Radioactivity is best described as:

A. Removal of an orbital electron by a photon
B. Spontaneous transformation of an unstable nucleus
C. Bremsstrahlung produced in a tungsten target
D. The Hounsfield number of a high-Z insert

Напиши букву. Ответа и конспекта в этом сообщении нет.

ЛИКА
B

БОТ
Конспект после оценки · Radioactivity

B. Spontaneous transformation of an unstable nucleus

Radioactivity is a nuclear change (alpha, beta, electron capture, gamma, and related modes). Knocking out an orbital electron is ionization. Bremsstrahlung is an x-ray process. The Hounsfield number is a CT attenuation scale.
